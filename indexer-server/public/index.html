How this works

A staked indexer watches CLAWD

An indexer staked USDC on the IndexerRegistry contract to register the (CLAWD, Transfer) event pair. Wrong answers can be disputed and slashed — the stake is the bond.

You sign, you don't gas

Clicking a question asks your wallet for one EIP-712 signature — no transaction, no gas. The signature authorizes a single $0.10 USDC settlement. Approve once, ask many times.

The indexer submits your signature to settleQuery: 98% pays the indexer, 1% the protocol treasury, and 1% buys $CLAWD and burns it. Every question shrinks supply.
